The `urna tui` ask tab opens the corpus once, offers its labels as you type (our own label index), lays out the selected hit from the manifest schema and shows its stored AVIF image, decoded in the tree (`urna-images` over `urna-av1dec`, a pure Rust fork of rav1d 1.1.0) and drawn through kitty, iTerm2 or sixel, or block characters elsewhere. `urna tui FILE --snapshot QUERY` and `--eval N` check it without a terminal.
