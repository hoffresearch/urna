The local gate runs what CI runs: `rust-toolchain.toml` pins the Rust of every build (1.100.0), `tool/tasks/fullcheck.sh --tier quick|push|full` runs in tiers, the push tier runs CI's commands on a clean worktree of the commit, and `tool/tasks/gatesteps.tsv` maps every step of `gatecheck.yml` and `lintworks.yml` to its local step or to the reason it has none. A test that lacks an external resource names the need it lacks (`tool/tests/testneeds.tsv`): the gate fails on a need it provides itself (`URNA_REQUIRE_<NEED>`), lists the others, and does not remember a step that skipped as passed. The scheduled miri job runs each test in a process of its own and ends with a verdict.
