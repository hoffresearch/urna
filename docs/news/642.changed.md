Ten third-party crates left the build, each replaced by a few lines of urna's own: `rayon` (unused in `urna-engine`), `rand` (a splitmix64 in `urna benchmark`), `coolor` (the xterm-256 fold in `cmd/tone.rs`, checked against coolor in `rust/clitui/parity`), `hex` (`urna_format::to_hex` and `from_hex`), `vergen-gitcl` (`build.rs` runs `git rev-parse --short HEAD`), `sysinfo` (the total ram from `sysctl`, `/proc/meminfo` or `GlobalMemoryStatusEx`), `thiserror` (the five error types write `Display` and `Error` by hand, with the same messages), `assert_matches` and `cfg-if` (in `urna-av1dec`) and the unused `tempfile`. `Cargo.lock` went from 311 to 285 packages. The 256-color fold now uses xterm's own greys for indexes 241 and 242, which coolor's table had off the ramp; two of 4096 grid colors fold differently, none of the ui palette.
