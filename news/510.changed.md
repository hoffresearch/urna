The workspace lints add `unsafe_op_in_unsafe_fn`, `missing_debug_implementations` and `rust_2018_idioms` (rustc) and `dbg_macro`, `todo` and `expect_used` (clippy), all `deny` (#510, part of #511). The paths that hid a lifetime name it (`Cursor<'_>`). Every public type is `Debug`: the thirteen that hold file bytes, the mmap or corpus-sized vectors (`MmapUrnaFile`, `HnswIndex`, `Bm25Index`, `CsrIndex`, `Traversal`, `UrnaView`, `UrnaFileBuilder`, `CsrParts`, `Deduped`, the int8/int4 views, `TxtStreams`, `IntpackReader`) print their shape through a crate-private `shape_debug!`, which names every field (`show`, `len`, `some` or `skip`) and destructures them all, so a field added later does not compile until it is named; the rest derive it. The two benches and one proptest helper allow `expect`. `rust/format` forbids `unsafe_code` (the engine's SIMD keeps the workspace from doing so). `rust/ingest` and `fuzz/`, workspaces of their own, carry a copy of the tables (`rust/ingest` also `unsafe_code = "forbid"` and `rust-version = "1.85"`); `tool/tests/test_lintmatch.py` (4 cases, in fullcheck, CI and the ruff list) holds both copies to the root's, and CI runs Clippy on `fuzz/` on stable. CONTRIBUTING, SECURITY and the atlas list the eight lints.
