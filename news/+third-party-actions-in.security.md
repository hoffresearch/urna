Third-party actions in `gatecheck.yml`, `wheelmake.yml` and `pypiindex.yml` are pinned to a commit SHA, with the version in a comment, so a moved tag upstream cannot change what runs, the PyPI publish action included. Repository settings to match: a `release-tags` ruleset refuses creating, moving or deleting a `v*` tag to everyone but admins, who bypass it, and the `pypi` environment deploys only from `v*` tags, `testpypi` only from `main`. `main` is guarded by the `opensource` ruleset alone: the classic branch protection that duplicated it is gone, and its two rules of its own (an up-to-date branch, resolved conversations) moved into the ruleset.
