urna installs with scoop (#563): `scoop bucket add hoffresearch https://github.com/hoffresearch/scoop-urna`, then `scoop install urna`. The `scoopship` job, after the release is announced, renders `pkgs/scoop/urna.json` with `pkgrender.py` from the release's own zip and SHA-256 and pushes it to the bucket with a token scoped to it, the way the Homebrew job pushes the formula; a manual dispatch publishes an existing tag. The manifest also carries `checkver` and `autoupdate`.
