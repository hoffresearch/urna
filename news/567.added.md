`urna setup` names the channel a binary came from and, where the channel has one, the command that updates it (#567): besides Homebrew, npm, cargo and the install script, it recognises scoop, winget and Chocolatey (by the paths their shims launch), a conda environment (by its `conda-meta/`, so a miniforge under Homebrew still reads conda), nix, and `/usr/bin/urna` owned by dpkg or rpm (apt or dnf; a `.deb` gets no update command until an apt source serves urna). The release report checks the release's `.deb` and `.rpm`, conda, scoop, COPR, Chocolatey and winget, with a version still under review by Chocolatey's or winget's moderators shown as in moderation rather than missing, and `setuptest` installs from conda, scoop, the `.deb` and COPR and checks that setup names each one.
