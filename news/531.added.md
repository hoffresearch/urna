`urna setup` offers a sample corpus after verify (#531, part of #535): the potion build of the fakenews-ptbr benchmark's release the catalogue names (v0.1, about 20 MB of Brazilian Portuguese news, true and fake side by side), downloaded from Hugging Face at a pinned dataset commit, refused unless its SHA256 is the catalogue's and `urna validate`'s checks pass, and saved as `<data root>/urna/sample/fakenews.urna`. It is optional like the models (a warning under "urna is ready", exit `16`), on by default, skipped by `--no-sample`, removed by `--uninstall`. The plan, the done screen, the `--yes` output and the README describe it as true and fake news and point at it instead of a checkout. `tool/tasks/upstreams.py --sample <release>` resolves the dataset's commit once, reads the file's size and the release's `SHA256SUMS` at that commit and refuses a release built with another `model_hash` than the payload's potion; the catalogue copies the entry and `catalogue.py --check` refuses a stale one, offline. `cleanhost.sh` validates the sample and asks it on the clean machine. `cmd::validate::check` runs `urna validate`'s checks without printing.
